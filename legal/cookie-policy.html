Cookie Policy

Version: 1.0

This policy explains how and why we use cookies on our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

  • Cookies are small files stored on your device to improve your browsing experience.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep the website running smoothly.
  • Remember your preferences.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Understand how visitors use our website.

3. Types of Cookies

  • Essential Cookies – Required for the website to function.
  • Performance Cookies – Help us improve website performance.
  • Analytics Cookies – Show us how visitors interact with our website.

4. Your Choice

  • You can accept, reject, or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • Disabling some cookies may affect certain website features.

5. Policy Updates

  • We may update this Cookie Policy when needed.

6. Contact

For questions about cookies or privacy, please contact our support team.
